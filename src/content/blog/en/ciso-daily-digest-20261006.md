## Meta and Microsoft Restrict Claude: Strategic Shift in AI Labor

Both Meta and Microsoft are actively limiting employee access to Claude as competitive pressures mount in the AI infrastructure race. The move signals broader concerns about dependency on Anthropic-powered tools and cost implications.

### Why This Reshapes AI Workforce Strategy

The restriction reflects a critical gap in vendor diversity for enterprise AI tooling. With Claude's rapid adoption in banking (Barclays), healthcare, and government, workforce access controls represent the first regulatory-style friction point. Organizations face a choice: lock down external AI dependencies or build internal alternatives (DeepSeek, local models).

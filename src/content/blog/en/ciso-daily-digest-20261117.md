### 📌 Apple previews finer macOS permissions for app and data access

Apple is testing more granular permissions in macOS 27.3 beta 2. The controls cover how apps, including apps that use AI agents, gain access to data or control other apps.

The preview lets users limit certain permissions to specific apps or folders, gives users more visibility into what broader access covers, and offers an option to ask each time access is needed.

Apple said existing grants will remain in place. Security teams can use the beta to test whether approved workflows still work with narrower permissions before updating their deployment guidance.

**Source:** [Apple Tightens macOS App Permissions to Limit Data Access Amid AI Agent Security Risks](<https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/apple-tightens-macos-app-permissions-to.html>)

### 📌CISO Impact

- **Review existing grants —** Inventory apps with Full Disk Access or permissions to control other apps and document why each grant is needed.
- **Pilot narrower access —** Test folder-level or app-level permissions against representative endpoint workflows before broad deployment.
- **Check critical workflows —** Include backup, assistive technology, and AI agent workflows when testing the effect of narrower permissions.
